Butter Chicken

Succulent chicken pieces cooked in a creamy tomato and butter sauce. Perfectly spiced for rich flavor.

Our Butter Chicken is a classic Indian dish where tender chicken is marinated, grilled, and simmered in a luscious tomato-based gravy with cream and butter. Served with naan or rice, it’s a comforting and indulgent meal loved by everyone.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken pieces (boneless or bone-in)
  • Tomatoes, cream, and butter
  • Onions, garlic, and ginger
  • Spices: garam masala, chili, turmeric, coriander
  • Fresh cilantro for garnish

Nutrition Info (per serving):

  • Calories: 420 kcal
  • Protein: 28g
  • Carbs: 12g
  • Fat: 30g
