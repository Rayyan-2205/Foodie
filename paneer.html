Kadhai Paneer

Soft paneer cubes sautéed with colorful bell peppers and rich kadhai masala.

Our Kadhai Paneer is a flavorful vegetarian delight where fresh paneer cubes are cooked in a tangy tomato-onion gravy, tossed with capsicum, and seasoned with traditional Indian spices. Perfect with naan, roti, or steamed rice.

Ingredients:

  • Paneer cubes (cottage cheese)
  • Onions, tomatoes, and bell peppers
  • Ginger, garlic, and green chilies
  • Kadhai masala (coriander, cumin, red chili, turmeric)
  • Fresh cream and cilantro for garnish
