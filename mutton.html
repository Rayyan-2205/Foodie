Mutton Curry

Tender mutton pieces slow-cooked in a rich, spicy gravy.

Our Mutton Curry is a hearty dish made from succulent mutton pieces, marinated with traditional Indian spices, and cooked slowly to perfection. The gravy is rich, flavorful, and perfect to pair with steamed rice, naan, or roti for a fulfilling meal.
